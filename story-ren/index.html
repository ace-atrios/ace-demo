About Atrios · Founder's story

I started Atrios because I was drowning in FOMO.

Every day, a new tool. Every day, another cold call. I knew there had to be a better way.

Taylor OfferFOUNDER · ATRIOSPortrait with a sealed recommendation, oil on canvas
I

Too many tools

01

As a founder, I’ve always felt like I’m falling behind.

Every morning, another company launches something that’s supposedly going to change everything. A new AI agent. A better CRM. A smarter way to run payroll, banking, sales, hiring, or accounting.

Some products are actually game-changing. Some really suck. The right tool can save you hundreds of hours, replace an entire workflow, or give you an edge over competitors. The wrong one can cost you years.

02

But how do I know which tools are good?

Every company claims to be the best. Every ad promises to change your life. Review sites feel like reviews people paid for. And nobody has time to sit through 50 demos just to pick software. I certainly didn’t.

03

So I asked people I trust.

I texted my friends. I asked other founders. I called people I respected and asked what they were using. Almost every time, the best products I found came from those conversations.

Not from an ad. Not from a cold email. Not from a salesperson.

From someone I trusted.

The best tools come from people we trust. Yet companies keep trying to reach us with spam.

II

Too much spam

01

The harder I looked, the more people tried to sell me.

My inbox flooded with automated emails. My phone wouldn’t stop ringing (I’m on Do Not Disturb permanently now, thanks to salespeople calling every 5 minutes). LinkedIn became a graveyard of AI-slop sales pitches.

Every company wanted 15 minutes of my time. Every salesperson was convinced that calling, emailing, and texting me 17 times was the way to get me to try their product.

02

Something was fundamentally broken.

I wanted to discover the best products in the world. Companies wanted to get their products in front of people like me. We both wanted the same thing.

Yet the system designed to connect us was making it miserable for everyone.

03

The best product wasn’t winning.

The loudest (and often most annoying) sales team was. I was spending more time avoiding salespeople than discovering products that could actually help my business.

How backward is that?

That spam isn’t free. Companies spend billions on it every year.

III

Follow the money

01

Companies spend billions to get our attention.

Billions flow to Google, Meta, sales teams, ad agencies, cold-email software, and every platform designed to fight for our attention. All of it spent trying to convince us to buy something.

And what do we get? More ads. More emails. More interruptions.

02

Then it hit me.

Instead of spamming us with billions, what if they just gave that money to us?

Instead of paying Google to show me another ad, why not pay me to discover a product that could actually help my business?

Instead of spending thousands convincing me to switch, why not reward me for switching to something better? Instead of paying strangers to spam my inbox, why not reward the people I trust for recommending products they love?

03

The money was already there.

Companies would still acquire customers. Customers would discover better products. And the people creating real value would finally get rewarded.

We just needed to change where it went.

So I built a way to send that money to the people instead.

IV

So I built Atrios

01

The best products should win.

I started Atrios with a simple belief: the best products should win, and the money should go to people, not to spam.

Instead of companies spending billions interrupting people, that money flows directly to the people they’re trying to reach.

02

Get rewarded to discover.

Get paid to discover a product that makes your business better. Get rewarded for switching to a tool that saves you time or money. Unlock perks, credits, and discounts just for trying something new.

03

Get rewarded to recommend.

Earn money simply by recommending a product you already love to someone who needs it.

That’s Atrios.

Atrios pays you to discover great products. Ace finds the right ones for you.

V

Meet Ace

01

Your friend who knows every tool.

Ace is your personal AI agent for finding the best tools for your business. Think of it as the friend who has talked to every business, reviewed every product, and knows every founder.

02

It learns what you actually need.

It learns about your company and what you’re trying to accomplish. It finds what founders, operators, and people you trust are really using, cuts through the noise, and brings you only what’s worth your attention.

03

And gets you rewarded for it.

When there’s a great opportunity, Ace helps you get rewarded for exploring it. No endless research. No guessing. No cold calls.

Text Ace whenever you want.

And it’s bigger than software. It’s a better way for the world to buy and sell.

VI

The future of sales doesn’t have to suck

01

A better way for the world to buy and sell.

Think about how much of our lives we spend being sold to. Every website. Every feed. Every inbox. Every phone call.

Somewhere along the way, we accepted that this is just how business works. It doesn’t have to be.

02

Trust becomes more powerful than advertising.

The best products spread because people genuinely love them. Your relationships, recommendations, and attention are recognized as valuable. And companies stop chasing customers, because their happiest customers become their greatest advocates.

Buyers aren’t targets. They’re partners.

03

The best products win because they’re the best.

Not because they have the biggest ad budgets. Discovering something new feels like a tip from your smartest friend, and every great recommendation creates value for the person giving it, the person receiving it, and the company behind the product.

The Atrios Declaration

VII · In the year 2026

Let’s change the way the world sells

✦

What started as a personal frustration has turned into something much bigger.

Millions of people want to discover better products and get rewarded for the value they create. Millions of companies are building incredible products that deserve to be discovered.

We just need a better way to connect them.

That’s what we’re building at Atrios: taking an industry built on interruption and rebuilding it around trust, and putting the money that used to go to ads and spam into the hands of real people.

It won’t happen overnight. But every great product discovered, every recommendation rewarded, and every cold call replaced with a real connection brings us one step closer.

The future of sales doesn’t belong to the companies that spend the most to spam us. It belongs to the companies we trust, the products we love, and the people who bring us together.

And I’d love for you to be part of it.
