Easy pace should feel embarrassingly slow. If you can't talk in full sentences, slow down. Most of your miles are easy; that's how beginners get fast without getting hurt.

Race plan (Glacier course)

Miles 1–5: ~900 ft climb. Run by effort, ~10:00/mi. Don't chase pace here.

Miles 5–10: downhill past Two Medicine Lake. Bank time, ~8:40/mi.

Miles 10–13.1: rolling hills. Hold ~9:00 and finish strong.

Course is ~4,800 ft altitude. Expect to feel it. Arrive 2+ days early if you can.

Rules

• Sharp pain = stop and rest. Soreness is fine, pain isn't.

• Missed a day? Skip it, don't double up.

• Your gym work counts: keep it, just keep heavy legs 48h+ before long runs.

• Long runs are about time on feet, not speed.

• Tune-up 10K under 55:00 in late April = on track for sub-2.
