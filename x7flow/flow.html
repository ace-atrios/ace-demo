One perks experience, three ways in

Today "get perks", "share perks" and "create a stack" are separate onboarding branches. In this version they collapse into one thing: a perks page you can act on right away. Every route lands on something useful in one step, and every perk opens the same panel with two actions: claim it or share it. Vendor signup stays its own path. Click any box to see that screen.
