About Atrios · Founder's story

I started Atrios because I was drowning in AI tools.

And I had the worst FOMO of my life.
Taylor Offer · Founder
I.

A new tool every morning

I spent 10 years as the founder and CEO of Feat. When I started building again, everything had changed.

Every morning there was a new AI tool. A new launch on X. A new “this changes everything” thread. A friend in a group chat asking, “Wait, you're still using that?”

I'd sign up for a free trial, forget about it, and sign up for three more. My browser had more tabs than my company had employees.

“Am I using the right tools? Is my competitor using something better? What am I missing?”

I didn't know what my business should be using. And I couldn't shake the feeling that someone, somewhere, was moving faster because they'd found the thing I hadn't.

That's FOMO. And for founders right now, it's constant.
II.

Keeping up became a full-time job

Here's what I realized: the companies with the best tools win. A small team with the right stack moves like a team ten times its size.

But figuring out the right stack had become a full-time job. Reviews are paid for. Rankings are sponsored. Every vendor says they're the best.

The only advice I actually trusted came from friends. Founders a few steps ahead of me who'd say, “Use this. Skip that. Here's why.”

Most founders don't have a friend like that for every decision. I didn't either.

III.

I had been that friend for years

The funny part is that I'd been that friend for other people for a decade. Whenever I met someone, I'd ask:

“How can I help you?”

Then I'd introduce them to the right people, and ask the company what they could offer my friend that they couldn't get anywhere else. A better price. A free trial. Real access.

Companies started competing to give my friends better deals, because a trusted introduction beats any ad. One even told me 70% of their revenue had come from my introductions, and insisted on paying me for it.

So I asked myself: what if everyone had that friend? For every tool, every decision, every day?
IV.

So I built it

Atrios takes in thousands of signals: what founders actually recommend to each other, what similar companies buy, funding and hiring news, and honest feedback from real intro calls.

It learns what your business does, then tells you the best tools for you, and why. And because companies want to meet customers through someone they trust, it gets you perks you can't get on your own: credits, cash bonuses, free months and real discounts.

You can just text Ace, our AI, and ask: “Who should we bank with?” “How do we hire our first engineer?” You get an answer in seconds.

Always free for you. You never pay a dollar.
V.

Peace of mind

These days, I don't lie awake wondering if I'm missing something. I know I'm using the best tools for my business, because Atrios is always looking for me.

That's what I want for every founder: to stop chasing every launch, stop second-guessing, and get back to building.

Stop working like a caveman. Let Atrios find the best tools for you.
