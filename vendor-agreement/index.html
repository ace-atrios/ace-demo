What Atrios does for you

Atrios puts your product in front of the founders and operators who fit your ideal customer, at the moment they need it, and books qualified meetings on your calendar. Every recommendation comes from someone they trust: our AI agent, a tastemaker in their network, or a community they belong to.

How meetings work

  • Every lead is screened against your criteria first. You only pay for qualified meetings, and screened-out leads are never billed.
  • You get an alert for every booking and can cancel any meeting for free before it happens.
  • Leads commit to showing up and giving honest feedback. Ghosters aren't paid and lose access.
  • Dispute any meeting or conversion within 24 hours if it didn't match your criteria.

Your terms

Price per meeting
$ per qualified meeting

What you pay for each meeting with a lead who fits your criteria. For example, $500 per meeting with a founder or head of finance at a US company with 20+ employees. You can change it anytime by message.

Conversion bonus
once per customer, when the deal is closed-won

Charged only when a meeting turns into a closed-won deal, once per customer. For example, $2,000 per new customer, or 10% of first-year contract value. Atrios shows which deal it applies to.

Perk

An offer for leads who take a meeting with you. For example, 3 months free, $500 in credits, a free benefits benchmark, or a free security audit. Atrios tells each lead what it is and reports how many claimed it.

Ideal customer (ICP)

Atrios only books leads who fit this, and refines it with you.

Buying triggers

Moments when someone is most likely to buy. We train our agent on these and recommend you when they happen.

Platform fee
$2,000 a month, billed annually ($24,000 for the term). Covers everything Atrios provides above.
Prefunded balance
$10,000, invoiced at signing

Qualified meetings draw from a prepaid balance, so tastemakers are paid right away. Unused balance rolls over.

Replenishment
When your balance drops below $5,000, your card on file is charged $5,000 to refill it.
Max spend per month
$ including replenishments

The most you will be charged in a month. When you reach it, meetings pause until the next month or until you raise it.

Prices, criteria and perk can be updated anytime by message in your Atrios channel.

What you provide

  • An honest description of your product, buyer and offer.
  • Attend booked meetings on time, allow the Atrios notetaker to join, and fill in the short feedback form.
  • Keep rewards inside Atrios: don't pay tastemakers or leads directly for introductions made here.

Term

12 months from signing. It renews for another 12 months unless either side cancels in writing at least 3 months before the end of the term.
