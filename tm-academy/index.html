The 6 steps

1

Pick a big payer

Start with companies paying $330-$500 a call.

2

Only people who clearly meet the must-haves.

3

Send one short message

Email or LinkedIn. Money up front, must-haves as a list.

4

Same thread, ~1, 2, then a few days later.

5

cc Ace on "yes"

Ace qualifies, books, reminds and credits you.

Stack more calls

Ace offers them 2-3 more paid calls.

Why it works

  1. Money in the subject line
  2. One credibility line about the company
  3. "Paid whether or not you switch"
  4. Must-haves as a short numbered list
  5. Ask for times, then cc Ace

Rules that keep the money flowing

Only people who truly fit. Misrepresented calls pay no one.
They show up on time, camera on, engaged.
Never promise what the company didn't offer.
Credit for every meeting in their first 30 days.
