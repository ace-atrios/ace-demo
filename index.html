Ace does the rest.

Ace is the Atrios AI teammate. A tastemaker texts "this is my friend, they need X." Ace researches the friend, matches them to the vendors they actually qualify for, sends the perks and why each one fits, and books the call. No forms, no links to explain.

How it works
Ace TriosTastemaster @ Atrios · works over iMessage, SMS, email and Slack

What Ace is doing behind the scenes

1

Identifies the friend from a name, number or email: public web research on role, company, stage, funding, headcount and hiring.

2

Scores every vendor 0-100 against each vendor's own qualification rules (funding, revenue, team size, location, intent).

3

Sends only the fits, each with the lead-facing perk and one line on why it helps their business.

4

Books the call and drops a research brief in the vendor's Slack, so the AE walks in prepared.

Why it works

Tastemakers told us the link-and-form flow felt awkward and made them look like a vendor's sales rep. Texting a friend's name to someone who handles it is how intros already happen.

For tastemakers

One text, zero homework

No collections to build, no links to explain. Ace speaks for you warmly, and you get credit for the intro.

For the friend

Perks first, no info wall

They see what they get and why it fits before anything is asked of them. Booking is a reply with a few times.

For vendors

Pre-qualified, pre-briefed

Only leads who clear your ICP get offered. Every meeting arrives with a research brief and a fit score.
