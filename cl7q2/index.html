Lead acquisition

GoalBook a qualified lead into a paid 30-min call with one vendor.
WhoThe operator who owns the decision (Head of People, Finance or Ops, or the CEO under ~20 people) at companies that pass the vendor's bar.
TriggerAlways start from a signal: benefits renewal (Form 5500), new role in the last 90 days, new funding round, or a relevant job posting.
Built fromIgnition new-leader (email + LinkedIn, 12-20% reply), Corridor renewal (most positive replies), and the 6.6x reply lift from adding LinkedIn.
