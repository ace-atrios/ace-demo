Get paid to book meetings

The exact outbound playbook our top tastemaker uses to book 20+ meetings a week. Companies on Atrios pay for 30-minute intro calls with the right people. You find them, offer them cash for their time, and Ace handles everything after "yes".

1Pick one company with a big payout

Start with the top payers. Every call that happens pays the person who takes it and you.

  • withCoverage (business insurance, companies with $20M+ revenue): $500 to them
  • Corridor (health benefits, 5+ US W-2 employees, renewal in the next 3 months): $500 to them
  • Rho (startup banking, $500K+ raised and in the bank): $330 to them + $875 if they switch

See every company, what you earn per call and who qualifies on the Atrios companies page: atrios.com/tastemaker/companies ↗

2Build a list of people who clearly fit

Use the company's must-haves as your filter. Go for the decision maker: CEO, CFO, COO, Head of People or Finance.

  • Best timing: just raised, new in role, renewal coming up, hiring
  • Ask yourself "would this company be excited to meet them?", not just "will they take a call?"
  • Mid-size, real businesses convert best. A CFO at a 50-person manufacturer is a better at-bat than a 2-person startup

3Send one short email or LinkedIn message (copy these)

One company per message. Money up front. One credibility line. "Paid whether or not you switch." The must-haves as a short list. Ask for times.

6Stack after the first call

After a call, Ace offers them 2-3 more companies they fit. One yes often turns into three paid meetings, and each one pays you too.

Rules that keep the money flowing

  • Only reach out to people who truly meet the must-haves. Calls that turn out to be misrepresented don't pay anyone.
  • They need to show up on time, camera on, and engaged.
  • Never promise anything the company didn't offer. If you're unsure, ask Ace.
  • Keep it honest: "paid whether or not you switch" is the deal, and no fake deadlines.
