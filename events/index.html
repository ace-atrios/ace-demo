How RSVP works

No forms. Just text Ace.

The RSVP is a two-minute conversation. Guests get on the list, and every one of them becomes a real Ace conversation with intent we can act on.

1
Tap "RSVP with Ace"

Opens iMessage with the event code pre-filled (QR on desktop). Because they text first, links and calendar invites land clean.

2
Ace asks 3 things

Name + company, what they're building, and one thing on their plate this quarter (hiring, raising, benefits, sales…).

3
On the list, or host approval

Open events confirm instantly. Curated dinners go to the host's approve/waitlist queue with Ace's fit score.

4
Before + after the event

Calendar invite, day-of reminder, "who's coming" for opted-in guests. After: 1-2 perks that match what they told Ace, booked by text.

What we get that Luma doesn't

Every guest is a conversation

Today a Luma RSVP is a form fill we never hear from again. Here, every guest has an open thread with Ace, so the post-event follow-up is a reply, not a cold message.

Intent before they walk in

Needs captured at RSVP ("hiring 2 engineers", "renewing benefits in Jan") feed the matcher. Vendor sponsors see a pre-qualified room.

Tastemaker credit built in

"Who invited you?" is part of the convo, so community hosts and tastemakers get credit for the guests they bring and any meetings that follow.
